04 Google Meet audio routing setup

STEP 1 · IN GOOGLE MEET (MEET.GOOGLE.COM)
Settings → Audio Options
In your Google Meet call, open Settings → Audio and select these exact names:
🎙️
Google Meet Microphone
EmotionCast (Google Meet Microphone)
Picks up your translated speech so other meeting participants hear you.
🔊
Google Meet Speaker
EmotionCast (Google Meet Speaker)
Sends incoming meeting speech into EmotionCast so it can be translated.
STEP 2 · IN EMOTIONCAST APP (CHANNELS ABOVE)
Desktop Studio Channel Assignments
Configure the channels in sections 02 and 03 (or click ⚡ Auto-Route Everything):
🗣️
Speak Channel (Section 02 · You → Meet)
Input: Your Physical Microphone (or System Default)
Output: ★ EmotionCast - To Meet Microphone
👂
Listen Channel (Section 03 · Meet → You)
Input: ★ EmotionCast - From Meet Speaker
Output: Your Real Headphones (or System Default)

Virtual devices are managed locally via PulseAudio / PipeWire. If you ever experience audio issues in Meet, click "⚡ Auto-Route Everything" to recreate and verify the virtual routes.
